## Our Commitment
Amora is a dating app strictly for adults aged 18 and over.
Zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE).

## Age Verification
All users confirm they are 18+ during registration.
Date of birth is collected and verified at onboarding.
Accounts belonging to minors are immediately suspended.

## Prohibited Content
- Any sexual content involving minors
- Grooming or exploitation of minors
- Sharing or requesting CSAM

## Reporting
Every profile has a Report button. Reports are reviewed promptly.
Accounts in violation are permanently banned.
CSAM is reported to NCMEC and national authorities as required by law.

## Compliance
Amora complies with the UK Online Safety Act, GDPR, COPPA,
Google Play Child Safety policy, and all applicable child protection laws.
